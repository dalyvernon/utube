    Frequently Asked Questions

    Is Aura Resume Builder ATS compliant?

    Yes. Aura Resume Builder uses a clean structure without tables in the resume body and focuses on ATS-friendly formatting.

    Does Aura Resume Builder process my data locally?

    Yes. Resume drafting, scoring, keyword analysis, and save scenarios are processed in your browser with local storage support.

    Can I export my resume to PDF and DOCX?

    Yes. You can export your resume to print-ready PDF and downloadable DOCX format directly from the app.

    Which languages are supported?

    Aura Resume Builder supports English, Spanish, French, German, and Hindi through built-in dictionary-based language switching.

    Does the keyword optimizer compare job descriptions?

    Yes. It extracts top keywords from a job description, calculates match percentage, and highlights missing terms.
